Glyphrun · weapons prototype

Four weapons for a hero who already holds sudo. Each seed has exactly one of them. Enemies are simplified stand-ins (see enemies.html for the real look) and cannot hurt you here.

Rules

  • One weapon per seed, drawn from the seed on its own random sub-stream, so it never shifts level generation. The same seed always gives the same weapon.
  • Getting it: collect a sudo bit while already big. You get the usual already root bonus and the seed's weapon, with its own toast and, for 4 s, a box under it: rubber duck acquired / press [X] to throw the duck. Firing once fades it out early. There is one sudo bit per level, so the hero has to carry sudo in from the previous level: level 2 at the earliest. The boss's [patch] arms a big hero the same way.
  • Losing it: any hit shrinks the hero to @ and takes sudo and the weapon. A pit or a lost life does too. To get the weapon back you need two sudo bits again. A sudo bit collected while armed gives score only.
  • Always big: an armed hero is always @@ (44 px tall). Every shot leaves low, 14 px above the feet, so straight shots still hit a bug on the ground. To hit something floating, you jump.
  • Fire: X (proposal). One press, one shot; holding does not repeat. Ammo is unlimited. Each weapon has a cooldown and a limit on how many of its shots can be on screen.
  • What dies: shots kill what stomping kills (bug, zombie, ping, hang, fork bomb, which splits just as it does when stomped). Traps (spikes, cron job, pipe daemon, dangling pointer, spinlock) absorb shots. The breakpoint is the exception. A kill scores and shows its message exactly like a stomp.
  • Not a shield: hits work as before, and the validator still proves every level without a weapon.

Weapons

Each demo is the same little range: a hang up high, a bug walking, a zombie floating at jump height, a ping. The hero jumps every few seconds and fires whenever the weapon is ready, so you can see what each one reaches and what it misses.
